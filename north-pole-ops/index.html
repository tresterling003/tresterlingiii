Sensitivity

Phones & tablets

Put your left thumb down anywhere on the left side and a joystick appears under it. Push it all the way forward to sprint. Drag anywhere on the right side to look. Hold the red Throw button to fire, and slide your thumb while holding it to aim at the same time. Tap the weapon box (top right) to swap weapons and the killstreak chip under it to call one in.

Aim slowdown (phones & controllers)

Off by default: every hit is your own aim. Light only slows your look speed a little while your crosshair crosses an enemy. It never moves your aim for you.
