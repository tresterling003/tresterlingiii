Welcome to the realm

Emberfall: Open Realm is an open-world adventure built on the rules of tabletop role-playing games like Dungeons & Dragons. You do not need to have played one. Everything is explained here, and the game does the maths for you.

You play one hero in a big world. Go anywhere: villages, forests, a swamp, mountains, ruins, and a haunted Barrow in the far north. Talk to people, take on quests, fight monsters, find treasure, steal horses (we will not tell)… or just ride around and watch the sun set.

There is a story — the stolen Ash Crown — but you can follow it whenever you like.

The d20: the heart of the game

Every time you swing, shoot or cast, the game rolls a 20-sided die for you and adds your attack bonus. If the total reaches the enemy's armor, you hit — then more dice decide the damage. You will see the rolls float up: 🎲14+5 −9.

  • A natural 20 is a critical hit: double damage dice, and time slows for a moment.
  • A natural 1 always misses. It happens to everyone.
  • Monsters roll against your armor too. Dodge-rolling makes you untouchable for an instant. Blocking soaks up most of a hit from the front.
  • Skill beats mashing. Every swing, block and roll costs stamina. Tap to attack (three quick blows chain into a combo), or hold and release for a heavy blow that smashes through a raised shield.
  • Enemies flash orange and show a "!" just before they swing. Raise your block right then to PARRY — no damage, and they stagger. Hit a staggered foe for a COUNTER: always a critical. Roll at the last instant for a perfect dodge — same reward.
  • Big attacks glow as a red circle on the ground first. Get out! If you are still inside, you make a saving throw — a d20 roll that halves the damage if you make it.

Checks, Fate and your hero

Out of combat, risky things — persuading a troll, picking a lock, picking a pocket — are checks: a big d20 roll plus one of your four abilities, against a target number.

  • 💪 Might strength · 🤸 Agility speed and stealth · 🧠 Mind magic and wits · 💬 Charm talking your way through.
  • ✨ Fate lets you re-roll a failed check. Natural 20s earn more.
  • Your class sets your health, armor, weapon and two special abilities. The second unlocks at level 3.
  • Defeat monsters and finish quests for XP. Each level gives more health and an ability point. Bosses and chests hold magic items that make you permanently stronger.

Life in the realm

  • Quests — people with a ! above them have work. The yellow ◆ marker and your minimap show where to go.
  • Shrines — touch the glowing stones to heal and unlock fast travel from the map.
  • Horses — buy one from Old Tom at the stables, or ride off on one that is not yours. Whistle (H) and your horse comes running.
  • Crime — hitting townsfolk, stealing or horse theft gets you wanted stars ★. Guards hunt you. Lose them by staying out of sight, pay your fine to Captain Hale, or talk your way out.
  • Day and night — wolves and the restless dead come out after dark. Glowcaps glow.

That is everything. Good luck, hero.
