How to play

Explore is endless: every depth is a new maze of poolrooms. Find the dark doorway that leads down. Listen for the low hum: it gets louder near the way down. Each depth is darker, wetter and less alone.

Story is six hand-built levels. Find the dark doorway out of each level. Some doors need power, or valves turned, before they open.

Something lives in the water. It hunts by sound. Walking in water makes noise; running makes a lot. It cannot climb onto dry tiles. Sneak through the water, and get out when you hear it coming.

Your flashlight helps you see, and helps it see you.

Your heart rate climbs near deep water, in the dark, and when things notice you. Too high and you black out. If you can't see the floor, it isn't there.

Collect the recordings left by the last person who came down here.

Keyboard: WASD move · Mouse look · Shift run · C sneak · Space jump · E use · F flashlight · Esc pause

Touch: drag left side to move, right side to look. Buttons for light, use, sneak, run and jump.

Headphones strongly recommended.
