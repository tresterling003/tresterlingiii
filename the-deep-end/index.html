How to play

Find the dark doorway out of each level. Stay on the tiles. If you can't see the floor, it isn't there.

Your heart rate climbs near deep water, in the dark, and when things notice you. If it gets too high, you black out.

Some things only move when you aren't looking at them.

Keyboard: WASD move · Mouse look · Shift run · Space jump · E use · F flashlight · Esc pause

Touch: drag on the left to move, drag on the right to look. Buttons to run, jump, use and light.

Headphones strongly recommended.
