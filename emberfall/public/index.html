EMBERFALL

Gather a party. Choose your path. Roll the dice.

Characters: KayKit by Kay Lousberg (CC0) · 3D: three.js

What kind of game is this?

Emberfall is a tabletop role-playing adventure, the same family as Dungeons & Dragons. There is no board and no score. You play one hero in a story, and the story changes with what you decide.

You are never against the other players. The whole party — you, your friends, and any computer companions — wins or loses together.

Each scene, the game describes what is happening and offers some choices. You pick one. Often, the dice decide whether it works.

Your hero

Every hero has four abilities. Each is a number, usually between −1 and +5, that gets added to your dice rolls:

  • 💪 Might — lifting, breaking, charging, intimidating.
  • 🤸 Agility — sneaking, dodging, lock-picking, aiming.
  • 🧠 Mind — magic, puzzles, medicine, noticing things.
  • 💬 Charm — persuading, bargaining, bluffing, befriending.

Your class (Knight, Mage, Rogue…) sets your starting numbers and gives you one special move. You also have health (how much hurt you can take) and armor (how hard you are to hit).

The d20: the heart of the game

When you try something risky, you roll a 20-sided die and add your ability. If the total reaches the target number, you succeed.

Picking a lock is an Agility check with target 13. Your Agility is +4. You roll a 10: 10 + 4 = 14. Success!

  • A natural 20 always succeeds, and earns the party a point of Fate.
  • A natural 1 always fails. It happens to everyone.
  • ✨ Fate lets you re-roll a failed check. It is scarce — spend it when it matters.
  • Advantage — when a companion who is good at the task helps, you roll two dice and keep the better one.

Failing is not losing. The story just goes a different, usually bumpier, way.

Battles

When a fight starts, everyone rolls initiative — a d20 plus Agility — and takes turns in that order, heroes and monsters mixed together. On your turn you choose one thing:

  • ⚔️ Attack — roll the d20, add your attack bonus, and try to reach the enemy's armor. If you hit, a second roll decides the damage. A natural 20 is a critical hit: double damage.
  • ★ Special move — your class's signature tricks. A few uses per battle.
  • 🛡️ Brace — much harder to hit until your next turn.
  • 🧪 Potion — heal yourself or an ally. Potions are shared by the party.

Some enemy magic hits everyone. Each hero then makes a saving throw — an automatic d20 roll — to take only half.

A hero at 0 health falls, but gets back up if healed, or when the fight is won. If the whole party falls, you try the battle again, refreshed.

Levels, loot and your hero sheet

  • ⭐ Levelling up — win the big fights and the party gains a level: more health, more special moves, a new power at level 2, and you choose an ability to raise.
  • Magic items — a burning sword, a ring of warding, a lucky coin. Whoever finds one keeps it, and it makes them permanently better.
  • 🪙 Gold buys potions and charms from traders, tolls from trolls, and songs from bards.
  • 📖 Hero — tap this at any time to see your character sheet: abilities, attack, armor, moves and items.

The Game Master — the game itself — describes each scene and plays every monster and villager. All you have to do is decide what your hero does next.
