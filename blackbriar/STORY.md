# Story bible — The Last Attendance (private chronology; players learn it in pieces)

**11 June 1998, Period 7.** A fire alarm fault triggers an overnight evacuation. Fourteen students in Room 014 (a basement overflow classroom off the east wing) are held back by the attendance officer, **Raymond Voss**, because the register "must be complete before anyone leaves." The wing's fire doors seal. The district later marks all fourteen ABSENT for the day — in a different pen, initialled R.V. — so the official record shows nobody was in the building. Room 014 is plastered over and removed from floor plans.
**Since then** the building replays that final school day. At 4:00 AM it takes the final headcount. Anyone outside a room is "unaccounted for" and is returned to one.
**The Monitor** is what remains of Voss: still carrying the register, checking doors, watching through classroom glass, reacting to disruption (noise), returning people to rooms. He cannot enter the maintenance office: it was never on the attendance system, and was reinforced after a 2003 contractor incident.
**Halberd Facilities** (the employer) knows enough to pay well and insist on the bolt. They want a cosmetic restoration that removes evidence, not an answer.

## Clues (consistent with the above)
- Night 1: *scratched name* under the Room 112 mud — "M. ARDEN 3B PRESENT 6/11/98" (official list: signed out 9:42 PM).
- Night 1: *attendance sheet* in locker 117 — all 14 of Room 014 ABSENT, initialled R.V.
- Night 1: *vent voice* — a register being read through the service grille, names in order.
- Night 2: *work order* for Room 014 (not on the plan) and the *painted-over 014 plate* by the chained west doors — points to the hidden wing.
## Longer arc (design only)
Restoration opens routes toward the sealed wing; evidence rebuilds the true register; Halberd's instructions become less trustworthy; the crew chooses between finishing the cover-up and completing the evacuation (answering the register for the fourteen).
