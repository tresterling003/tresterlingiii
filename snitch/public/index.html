SNITCH

One last job. One crew. Somebody's wearing a wire.

ROOM CODE

How to play

You're a crew of thieves on one last job — but some of you are Snitches, secretly working with the cops.

  • 🌙 Night: the Snitches pick one crew member to get busted. Special roles act in secret.
  • ☀️ Day: everyone talks, then votes on who to cut loose. A tie cuts nobody.
  • Crew wins when every Snitch is gone. Snitches win when they match the crew in numbers.

Roles

  • 🧤
    Crew — no power but your vote and your read on people.
  • 🔭
    Lookout — each night, tail one player to learn if they're a Snitch.
  • 🧰
    Fixer — each night, give one player an alibi that blocks a bust. Never the same player twice in a row.
  • 🎙️
    Snitch — knows the other Snitches, shares a secret channel, and picks who gets busted.
  • 🃏
    Fall Guy (6 or 8+ players) — wins alone by getting voted out during the day. Careful who you cut.

The twist: Lockup

Getting eliminated isn't the end. Players in Lockup get their own chat and each night smuggle out a 🪁 kite to one living player — whoever gets it has a double vote the next day.
