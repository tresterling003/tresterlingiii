## App direction

- RIVAL was retired because it was **confusing and complicated, and didn't feel modern or Apple-like.** First rule for the new app: simplicity, one obvious action per screen, native-iOS feel.
- **Design reference: Tonal mixed with Nike Run Club.** Screenshots in `private/design-refs/` (not committed; they're copyrighted marketing images). What stands out in them:
  - Huge, bold, italic condensed numerals as the hero of the screen (pace, distance).
  - One giant circular primary action (START).
  - Full-bleed athlete photography with bold uppercase titles.
  - Active-workout screens flooded with a single bright color.
  - Tonal-style workout summary: hero image, a row of three stats with a progress ring in the middle, a big total-volume number, exercise list with thumbnails and sets × reps × weight.
- Teaches graphic design, so may want to design the brand (name, logo) personally. To ask in the app round.

## Coach's notes (working)

- Strength base is high for someone 90 days off. Pull-ups and push-ups are already well above average.
- The biggest HYROX gap is running. 8 × 1 km under fatigue; current fresh mile is 8:30.
- **"Win my first HYROX" is a stretch goal and needs honest framing.** Age-group winners at large events tend to finish around an hour (verify against real results before setting targets). That takes roughly 6:50–7:15/mile runs after each station.
- **Race timing should fit the coaching calendar:** football season (Aug–Dec) is the busiest time; summer break is the best training block. A race 12 months out (Nov 2027) would land in football playoffs.
- **Burnout risk is real.** The interview describes the same all-in intensity that caused the football burnout of football. Plan hard weeks deliberately; don't make every day maximal.
- HYROX's appeal fits the football history: the clock decides the result, not politics.
- Consistency is the real limiter, not ability. Build the program and the app around adherence.
- Young kids at home plus a 4:30 wake time: sleep is probably the biggest recovery limiter. Ask.
- Jeans 33/34 at 215 lb suggests less fat to lose than "soft middle" implied. Get a tape measurement.
- Family runs with the wife are a built-in source of easy aerobic volume and accountability.
