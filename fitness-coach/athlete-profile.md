## Athletic background

- Trained for football from childhood until about 4 years ago (around age 24).

- How it ended: was cut and took it as the moment to stop. Was called back a few weeks later and chose not to return. Reason: **burnout**.
- Has done CrossFit. Has never seen or done HYROX.
- Self-directed consistency: longest stretch about 6 weeks; usually 1–2 weeks. What ended them: "a combination of all of it": schedule, motivation, and boredom.

RIVAL was retired because it was **confusing and complicated, and didn't feel modern or Apple-like.** The new app's first rule is simplicity: one obvious action per screen, native-iOS feel.

## Coach's notes (working)

- Strength base is high for someone 90 days off. Pull-ups and push-ups are already well above average.
- The biggest HYROX gap is running. The race has 8 × 1 km of running; age-group-competitive athletes hold roughly 4:30–5:15 per km under fatigue (about 7:15–8:30 per mile). Current fresh mile is 8:30.
- Consistency is the real limiter, not ability. Build the program and the app around adherence.
- A safety's build is a great match for both goals: muscular but fast.
- **Burnout is a design input.** Football ended because the grind stopped being worth it. Training and accountability must not recreate that. Make the tough modes about holding commitments, not grinding volume.
- Boredom was one of the reasons past streaks ended: build in variety and visible progress.
