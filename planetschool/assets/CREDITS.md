All third-party assets are public domain (CC0). No attribution is legally required, but credit is given gladly.

- **People**: [Ultimate Animated Character Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/animated-characters-pack) by
  [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com) (CC0). Recolored in-game with varied skin tones, hair and outfits.
- **Furniture & props (photo-scanned)**: [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) (CC0) - Mid-Century Lounge Chair,
  Modern Arm Chair, Modern Coffee Table, Potted Plants, Shrubs, Wall Clock, Security Camera, Steel Frame Shelves,
  Industrial Microscope, Chemistry Set, Wet Floor Sign, Outdoor Table & Chair Set.
- **Textures and sky**: [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) (CC0) - brick (tan, red, dark), painted plaster, facade panels,
  corrugated metal, polished concrete, terrazzo, hardwood, white oak, white tile, rubber track, asphalt, gravel roof,
  grass, and the Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky) HDRI.
- **Low-poly models**: [Kenney](https://kenney.nl) (CC0) - Nature Kit (trees, flowers), City Kit Suburban (houses),
  City Kit Roads, Car Kit (parked cars), Furniture Kit (toilets and kitchen pieces). License files are in each folder.

Everything else (walls, glass curtain walls, desks, chairs, smart boards, lab benches, bleachers, stadium,
stairs, elevators) is generated procedurally in code.
