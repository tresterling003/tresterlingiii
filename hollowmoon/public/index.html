HOLLOWMOON

A village. A campfire. Someone here isn't human.

Characters: KayKit by Kay Lousberg (CC0) · 3D: three.js

How to play

Hollowmoon is a village with a secret: some of the people around the fire are werewolves.

  • 🌙 Night: everyone sleeps. The werewolves choose someone to kill. The Sheriff and Doctor act in secret.
  • ☀️ Day: everyone talks, then votes to banish one person. A tie banishes nobody.
  • The village wins when every werewolf is gone. The werewolves win when they equal the villagers in number.

Roles

  • 🧑‍🌾
    Villager — no power but a vote and a good read on people.
  • ⭐
    Sheriff — each night, investigates one player and learns if they are a werewolf.
  • 💉
    Doctor — each night, protects one player from the wolves. Never the same player twice in a row.
  • 🐺
    Werewolf — knows the other wolves, shares a secret chat, and picks the night's victim.
  • 🃏
    Jester (6 or 8+ players) — wins alone by getting banished. Careful who you vote for.

The dead still play

Dead players become ghosts in the graveyard with their own chat. Each night they bless one living player, whose vote counts double the next day.
